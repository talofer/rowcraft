The Hidden Security Risks of Cloud Web-Scraping Extensions (<all_urls> vs activeTab)

Published by RowCraft · Updated November 25, 2026

When installing a browser extension to export HTML tables to Excel or CSV, most users click "Add to Chrome" without noticing the warning: "Read and change all your data on all websites." That permission (<all_urls>) allows background scripts to inspect banking portals, internal ERP dashboards, and private CRMs 24/7.

Why Strict activeTab + Local Memory Matters

  • Zero Background Access: RowCraft requests only activeTab, scripting, and storage. It cannot access any webpage until you explicitly click the toolbar icon or press Alt+Shift+E.

  • DOM XSS Hardening: Table previews are rendered strictly with document.createElement and textContent (zero innerHTML), preventing malicious table markup from executing inside the popup.
